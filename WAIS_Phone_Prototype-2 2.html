WAIS: Personal Budget Manager

Lab 5 UI/UX workbook. The fix for “where did my money go?”: every expense gets a category, a streak rewards complete logging, and the home dashboard shows the result.

Try it

The prototype opens on the lock screen. The default PIN is 1234, or use Google or continue without an account. Swipe the heatmap sideways to move between months.

Auto-lock

The P button on Home locks the app at once, so nobody can snoop. The app also locks by itself after 1 minute without a tap.

Locks in 60 s without a tap

Test mode

Start puts the phone on Home, already unlocked. Scenario: log a ₱180 lunch as Food & Dining, save it, then find the Food & Dining total in Summary. The timer stops when the Saved screen appears.
